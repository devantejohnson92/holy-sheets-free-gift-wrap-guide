HOW TO PRINT
- For the easiest, true-to-size printing, use the PDF files (US Letter or A4) and print at 100% / Actual Size.
- Open the PDF in a free PDF reader (such as Adobe Acrobat Reader), not a browser preview.
- Turn OFF “Fit to page.” Measure the 1-inch scale bar on each sheet before cutting.
- White cardstock, 65–110 lb (175–300 gsm), gives the best result.
- Cut along each piece’s cut outline, punch the hole with a 1/4 in hole punch and add ribbon or twine.
- Gift card sleeves (set 09): cut the outline, score and fold the dashed lines, glue the side tabs. Gift cards and cash not included.
- Gold and champagne tones are printed ink colors, not metallic foil. Colors vary by screen, printer and paper.
- To print a PNG on its own, place it in a document at the print size listed above (the files are 300 DPI at that size).
