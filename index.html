How to print your tags

  1. Print at 100% / “Actual Size.” Turn off “Fit to page” or “Scale to fit.”
  2. Use white cardstock (80–110 lb / 216–300 gsm). Matte gives the cleanest look.
  3. Choose High or Best quality and feed cardstock through the rear or manual tray if your printer has one.
  4. Check the 1-inch scale bar on page 3 with a ruler before you cut.
  5. Let the ink dry about 5 minutes, cut along the lines, and punch the hole with a 1/4 in hole punch.
